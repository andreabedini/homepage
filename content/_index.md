I have a PhD in mathematical physics and a few years of research behind me.
I'm based in Perth, Australia, and I work remotely for [Tweag](https://tweag.io)
as a software engineer — these days mostly making Haskell's developer
experience and build systems a little less painful.

I've been playing with computers since I was a teenager — early enough to have
watched the world change around them. I learned to program in BASIC when I was
ten, and my first paid job was as a Linux system administrator.

Since then: solution and cloud architecture, data science and engineering, and
now software engineering. Off the clock I solder — mostly resurrecting my kids'
toys — and I fall into deep rabbit-holes.

I organise the [Perth Functional Programmers Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/PerthFP/).
Back when I was in Melbourne, I started a science-based story-telling show
called [The Laborastory](https://thelaborastory.com).
